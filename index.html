Questions

Good to know

Does it read my messages or see what I watch?

No. It only notices when a short-video feed moves to a new video. It never reads captions, comments, messages or names, never takes screenshots, and everything stays on your phone.

Will it block Instagram or TikTok?

No. App blockers lock apps, and many people end up switching them off. This digital wellbeing app shows your real numbers and sends gentle reminders at 50%, 75% and 100% of your limit, so stopping becomes your decision.

How do I reduce my screen time on reels?

Measure first. Then set a limit about 20–30% below your normal day, turn on gentle reminders, keep a widget on your home screen, and plan something for the times you usually scroll, like after lunch or before sleep.

PAICONS Self-Development does the counting, limits, reminders and widget for you.

What is a dopamine detox? Can this help?

A dopamine detox usually means a break from quick, endless rewards like short videos, so slower things feel rewarding again. A sudden full stop rarely lasts. Cutting down gradually works better: know your number, then lower your limit week by week.

This is a self-awareness tool, not medical treatment. If phone use is seriously affecting your life, please talk to a professional.

Which phones does it support?

Android 10 or later, with Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels and TikTok. It needs Android’s Accessibility permission to notice a new video, explained clearly before you turn it on. iPhone isn’t supported yet.
